## Series identity

- Portrait 3:4, final raster 1086 × 1448 pixels.
- Warm off-white, subtly textured paper; clean, slightly irregular dark-brown ink outlines; light colored-pencil grain and restrained watercolor fills. Match the accepted Cat/Dog level of illustration detail. Keep forms readable at phone size; avoid a shift toward either flat corporate vector art or photographic realism.
- Main palette: cocoa/dark ink, warm coral branch lines, soft blush, pale sky blue, muted butter yellow, sage green. Let the new subject's natural colors appear without changing the series palette.
- Rounded, friendly English lettering. Use bundled Patrick Hand for exact text when typesetting; use bundled Noto Sans SC for Chinese. Do not rely on unbundled system fonts in portable production.
- Small fixed signature `新陈代谢（英语版）` near the bottom edge of labeled pages.

## Cover composition

Use [cover-art-direction.md](cover-art-direction.md) to plan topic identity, picture variety, shared hand-drawn rendering, and whole-page rhythm before accepting a cover. The approved reference images are full-page visual targets, not just sources of color or isolated icons. Use only `../assets/approved-window/cover.png` as the Window positive template for later home topics; earlier rejected Window drafts are not references.

- One dominant recognizable subject on the left or left-center, subject name nearby, with thin coral-red curved branch lines leading to ordered groups on the right. Match the accepted Carrot cover's **compact left-subject scale**: for a typical eight-group 1086 × 1448 page, the main subject itself (excluding title and connector lines) occupies about 40–45% of page height and no more than roughly the left third of page width. Adapt modestly for a different subject or group count while keeping comparable whitespace. Do not enlarge the left subject until it fills most of the page or shrink the right-side groups to compensate; preserve the established right-side heading, illustration and keyword scale. Compare the exported whole page with the Carrot size reference at phone width before acceptance. Derive branch count and endpoints from this topic's actual groups, not from the number of lines in a reference image. Construct the exact connector layer using [branch-topology.md](branch-topology.md); every visible curve serves one group and no spare line continues below the last group.
- On new topic covers, the first branch label is exactly `What`. Old approved cover images may show `What it is`; use them for composition and illustration style, never for that wording. Reserve a complete `What is a/an ...?` question for the Q&A pages.
- On new topic covers with a location branch, its heading is exactly `Where`. Put the full location question (for example, `Where do dolphins live?`) on the Q&A page, not the map branch. Old reference art with longer location headings controls only visual style.
- A group pairs one short heading with only the keywords needed for its learning point and clear literal mini-vignettes. **The numbered heading sits at the branch tip and leads immediately into that group's pictures, as in the accepted Cat/Dog covers. It may sit just above the first pictures, but must not form a detached vertical sidebar beside a distant picture grid.** Put each keyword directly beneath its corresponding picture. Some groups may need more/less space; do not force equal-height boxes. Keep text clear of fur, foliage, arrows and the next group. Do not omit a frozen-manifest fact merely to fit a predetermined icon count; revise the layout or split a requested format if needed.
- Use arrows for real actions and sequences. Avoid ornamental arrows that imply a false sequence.
- Color variation supports scanning; it must not replace readable text contrast.
- An animal may need vignettes for habitat, food, sound, behavior, senses, and stages. A vehicle may instead need parts, route, passengers, boarding, payment, and safety. Follow the subject.

## Picture-only practice

- Reuse the cover's **compactly sized** main subject, small vignettes, branch order, semantic grouping, and exact set of valid group connections. Compare its left-subject scale with the approved Carrot practice page; do not let the no-text version expand the main subject. Remove the heading/number circles entirely along with the text; the coral lines remain and end toward the relevant picture clusters, not at the old circle coordinates. Do not create a new decorative line.
- Delete every readable character, including labels embedded in signs, screens, bowls, icons, brand signature, digits, and ages.
- Preserve meaningful arrows and motion marks. Do not turn the page into a loose collage.
- Verify that a learner could point to each original group and say at least one sentence from visual cues alone.

## Bilingual Q&A

- English question and answer are larger and darkest. Chinese translation is smaller and quieter, but still legible at phone width.
- Match the approved **Dog** Q&A page family: stacked horizontal rows, numbered circles, soft blush/blue/yellow washes, dark handwritten English, smaller Chinese, and a generous illustration area on the right. Use one coherent illustration per row, integrated into the card. A pasted rectangular crop with its own background or border fails this requirement, even if its colors match.
- Follow the composition and checks in [qa-layout.md](qa-layout.md), whether producing the whole illustrated page at once or using an editable layout. Keep text and art in separate visual regions; no image may sit behind or touch a question, answer or translation.
- Continue across as many pages as the real content needs. No crowded final row, clipped sentence, or tiny font to preserve a page count.

## When visual consistency matters

Pass the specific reference images for the output type to image generation whenever image references are supported. Keep one recognizable subject design within a topic: after the topic cover passes review, use it as the subject reference for that topic's Q&A illustrations and video stills. Never carry key, cup, Hello Kitty, Cat, Dog, or their labels into an unrelated topic. Record the actual references, prompts and edits beside output for reproducibility. Generated art can vary across runs; exact text and geometry need a layout step and final inspection.
